# The Jester and the Six Houses

_Story content from `legacy/Story-TheJester.tex`, reformatted into sections and tables with spelling and grammar fixed. No content was added or changed. Painting 5 (The Alembic) is still to be written._

## Hidden story

Along the stories and paths the party travels, there will be pictures in discreet locations of the major Houses in the region.

The major Houses all have their own unique storylines that will give the party access to the pictures, though these pictures will not be obvious on all occasions.

The map of the area looks normal up front, but within the map of the region are hidden symbols within each area, and even a combination of symbols that join together into a greater symbolism.

The Clown / Jester / Joker / Comedian / Trickster (satyr) / Many-Faced God is always watching. If you anger it, it will attack. The party can't kill it, and can only get it to run away. It is the puppet-master that controls what it wants. It mostly leaves the Houses to whatever they want to do, but if a House tries to do something that actively goes against it, or causes too much information, it won't hesitate to take retribution.

## Spirit fights

### Winning a spirit fight
- They feel like they have just seen a glimpse of the future, ominous darkness.
- Gain some XP.
- Gain a token representing the image, useful later (potentially useful to show a weakness of another entity or versions of the boss). Tokens have no checks (they seem like just plain tokens).
- Potential value (low to high) depending on the vendor.

### Characters who die during a spirit fight
- Cursed with −1 to main stat (permanent, **not removable**).
- Stacks, up to −6 on main stat (permanent, **not removable**).
- Wake up from a bad nightmare, seem exhausted (lose short rests).
- The curse isn't visible: no warning or indicator, just a minus to the stat.
- Displayed minor debuff: −2 to a random proficiency (semi-permanent).

_Rule details for these, including how the lost short rests work: [house-rules.md](../house-rules.md#the-jesters-curses-spirit-fights)._

### Removing semi-permanent debuffs
A special questline or interaction removes these proficiency debuffs, probably unique to each different art fight. The lost-short-rest debuff is removed the same way, by a cleansing task.

## Examining the paintings

Different checks:
- Perception and Investigation both have levels.
- Bonus stat checks depend on the specific depiction.
- High rolls are required for unique flavour information (probably 15+).

Each tier below includes everything from the tiers before it ("additionally").

### 1) Kings Court — The Crown
_Artist unknown, oil painting. Face: Jester. Bonus checks: History, Insight._

| Perception | What you see |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Just a picture of a king's court dinner, nothing unique. |
| 4–7 | Additionally, you see everyone seems to be having a good time. |
| 8–10 | Additionally, you notice everyone is smiling, but something feels a bit off and you can't figure out why. |
| 11–13 | Additionally, you notice that while everyone is smiling, everyone's eyes look sad. |
| 14–16 | Additionally, you notice that the king's and queen's throats are crimson, like recently cut. |
| 17–19 | Additionally, you notice the queen is holding a knife in one hand with blood on it, and her other hand seems to be holding a goblet out towards the centre of the room. |
| 20 | Additionally, you notice everyone's eyes are focused on the jester, and there seems to be blood dripping from the table and instruments as well. Your eyes are drawn to the jester, and it appears that he is facing directly at you; as you move around the room he seems to follow you. |

| Perception | What you see |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | A pretty stained-glass window with vibrant colours. |
| 4–7 | Additionally, you notice some shapes: a large ball-like shape on the left, a small shape in the middle, a tall and skinny rectangular shape on the right, and a bunch of different coloured shapes around the edges. |
| 8–10 | Additionally, you notice that the shape on the left looks like a person kneeling, and the shape on the right looks like a person standing with some sort of head ornament. |
| 11–13 | Additionally, you notice the shape in the middle is a golden cup or chalice that appears to be handed from the right person to the left. The head ornament is actually a halo, and the person on the right has wings. |
| 14–16 | Additionally, you notice the shapes around the edges look more like small creatures, trying to reach toward the kneeling person. |
| 17–19 | Additionally, you notice that there appears to be a large creature (satyr) behind the person/angel on the right. It looks a bit like the creature is actually manipulating the person. |
| 20 | Additionally, you notice that the satyr (trickster) appears to be looking at you. You also notice small slivers of glass that stick out of the stained glass, which you can only really see from side angles. These slivers seem to come from the creature and wrap around the arms of the person on the right, and it almost looks like its hand is wrapped around the chalice too. |

| Perception | What you see |

| 4–7 | Additionally, you notice that the man is a hunter with some weapons. |
| 8–10 | Additionally, you notice that the hunter seems to be chasing some animals. |
| 11–13 | Additionally, you see that there is another small person on the edge of the tapestry that appears a bit goofy looking, carrying a sword bigger than itself. |
| 14–16 | Additionally, you notice the hunter's face actually looks scared and is looking back at the small person at the edge. The small person looks a bit like a clown: silly hat, puffy clothes and silly make-up on its face. |
| 17–19 | Additionally, the clown's face seems to be looking out at you. You also notice that the animals that looked to be chased by the hunter are actually laughing at the hunter. |
| 20 | Additionally, you notice that the clown's eyes seem to follow you as you look around the tapestry. The sword's edge seems to be woven from a glistening metallic fibre. As you look at it longer and longer, the fibres of the tapestry almost appear to be made of what they represent: the grass is made of grass, the fabrics feel like clothing, the animals' fur appears to be the fur of the animal it shows, and, even more bizarre, the skin of the hunter looks like actual human skin. |
